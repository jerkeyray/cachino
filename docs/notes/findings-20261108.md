# Findings and claims, 2026-11-08

Stage 3 of the [next stages plan](next-stages-plan.md): choose what the
project claims. It applies the research plan's stopping table to the results
and the [related work check](related-work-check-20261107.md). All engine results
come from vLLM `2173d712` on the Mac CPU with Qwen3 0.6B and 1.7B (and Qwen2.5
0.5B as a control), 32-token blocks. They concern token accounting, not latency.

## Main claim

> **From a raw capture of agent traffic, Cachino predicts how much prefix reuse
> each request gets on a pinned vLLM build, and attributes each request's lost
> reuse to its cause: prompt change, block rounding, eviction or sharing with
> other sessions.**

| Evidence | Result | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calibration probes | 17 of 17 steps as predicted | [M4](m4-cpu-results-20261107.md) |
| Capped-cache eviction probes | 27 of 27 exact | [E4](e4-cpu-results-20261107.md) |
| Sequential live sessions (four runs, two models) | Every turn exact | [live](live-cpu-results-20261107.md), [30 turns](live-readonly-results-20261107.md), [1.7B](live-1.7b-results-20261107.md) |
| Prompt replays | Exact, with prompt-ID parity on every request | same notes |
| Fixed trace at seven cache sizes | 30 of 30 exact at each | [sweep](cache-sweep-20261107.md), [stage 2](stage2-results-20261108.md) |
| Concurrent sessions, nine seeded runs plus three earlier | 259 of 265 exact in the matrix; misses only under eviction, by one to three blocks | [concurrent](concurrent-results-20261107.md), [stage 2](stage2-results-20261108.md) |
| Eviction predicted from capture alone | 9 of 9 runs classified; threshold confirmed at 487 and 488 blocks | [stage 2](stage2-results-20261108.md) |

**Withdraw or narrow if:**
- exactness fails on another vLLM build or on GPU (prompts up to 10,055 tokens
  were prefilled in 2,048-token chunks here and stayed exact);
- vLLM's native simulator, once merged, gives the same per-request numbers more
  cheaply, in which case Cachino is an attribution layer over it;
- overlap errors grow with more sessions or larger caches.

## Supporting findings

These are applications of the method. Both mechanisms are already known; the
measurements are the contribution.

**1. Eviction under shared load is the largest avoidable loss, and it has a
predictable threshold.** Concurrent sessions lost 17% to 25% of their
recomputation to eviction when their combined working set exceeded the cache,
and none otherwise. On one fixed trace, nothing was evicted above 488 blocks,
and eviction grew roughly steadily below that (43% of recomputation at 384).
The number of sessions alone did not predict it. The capture-based threshold
did. Known context: CacheWise, Continuum and Penelope build on this problem.

**2. The Qwen3 template boundary costs one reply per turn, and is caused by the
template.** With thinking off, every turn re-prefilled the previous reply:
7% to 19% of recomputed tokens across sessions, shrinking as observations grew.
A template without the cue (Qwen2.5) kept history aligned, and the engine
reused a 442-token reply's KV. Keeping the cue in Qwen3 history also restored
reuse, but changed the model's next action. Known context: Qwen3 issue #1826.

**3. In sequential single sessions that fit in the cache, most recomputation is
new content.** 64% to 91% of recomputed tokens, depending on observation size.
The remainder is the template boundary and up to one block of rounding.

**4. Rejected replies cause divergence regardless of template.** When the
harness drops a reply that failed its format check, the next prompt diverges at
the role marker.

## Not claimed

- Novelty for the template boundary or for eviction under agent load.
- Any latency figure; CPU timings are only directional.
- That these shares are typical of production agent traffic: one task, one
  harness and small models.
- That keeping the cue improves agents; it changed behavior and task outcomes
  were not measured.

## Stopping table applied

| Research plan rule | Applies | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No rendering or metric parity | No | Parity held everywhere it was checked |
| Harness is already append-only | Partly | With Qwen2.5 it is, apart from dropped rejected replies; kept as the control |
| Prompt fix helps only with ample cache | Yes | Reply reuse matters only while the cache keeps the reply; under eviction it can be lost anyway |
| Intervention hurts tasks | Unknown | The cue fix changed behavior; not adopted without a task-level test |
| Closest work covers the result | Yes | Positioned as measurement and attribution, with validated reproductions |
